# ADCToX v1.0.0

- **3-Stage Clinical & De Novo Workspace (`EN / KO`):** Integrated `Patient Profile`, `Recommend ADCs`, and `ADC Design` across web (`adctox`) and terminal (`adctox-cli`).
- **Approved ADC Safety Ranking:** Evaluates 15 FDA-approved ADCs safety-first against 120 DailyMed SPL rules (including Pediatric Section 8.4) and 32 pivotal clinical trials.
- **Multimodal `Auto Design` Engine:** Optimizes across 368,640 conjugate constructs and 5 Q3W doses using 5-fold `ADC-SurrogateNet-v2`, 252-step Q3W PK/PD ODE integration, and 64-draw QMC virtual-patient scoring.
- **Unified Cross-Platform Installers:** Full `v1.0.0` release bundles for Linux (`x86_64` / `arm64`), macOS (`Apple Silicon`), and Windows 10/11.
